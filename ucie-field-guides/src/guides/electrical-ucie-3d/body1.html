1 · What the electrical layer pins down

Interoperability

Any two compliant dies must link

Every device supports 4 GT/s and every rate between 4 GT/s and its maximum, so two dies with different maximums always share rates. Bump maps fix signal order so modules from different vendors line up.

Spec§5.1.1, p.184

Simplicity

Short, clean, single-ended

Single-ended, unidirectional, DC-coupled point-to-point lanes with a forwarded clock and length-matched routing within a module. The forwarded clock replaces clock recovery, and the short channel allows a small, low-power PHY.

Spec§5.0, p.184

Two families

Advanced (UCIe-A) vs Standard (UCIe-S)

Advanced Package (silicon bridge or interposer, e.g. EMIB, CoWoS): fine bump pitch, 2 mm reach, 64 or 32 lanes per module. Standard Package (organic substrate): 100–130 µm pitch, 10–25 mm reach, 16 or 8 lanes.

Spec§5.2.1, p.186 · Tables 1-1, 1-2, p.40

UCIe-3D

Stacked dies, almost no PHY

Hybrid-bonded stacks at ≤ 10 µm pitch run at up to 4 GT/s (the SoC clock) with no Adapter, no CRC or replay and a near-inverter PHY. The design goal is energy and area per bit, not reach.

Spec§6.2, p.258–259

x32 and x8 variants

An x32 Advanced module has 32 data lanes plus 2 redundant. An x8 Standard module has 8 data lanes. Up to 4 modules can be grouped for bandwidth.

Spec§5.3, p.190 · §5.2.1, p.186

Mixing x64 and x32

Optional for an x64 module. When used, the x32 always connects to the lower half of the x64; one wide module may not talk to two narrow ones at once.

Spec§5.7.2.4, p.228

Protect the receiver

Keep the Tx output high (VOH) within 100 mV above the receiving chiplet's front-end supply; an over-stress protection circuit may be used if it is higher.

4 · Clocking

Common reference clock

  • Both dies of a link use REFCLK from one source (package pin or forwarded by another die). 100 MHz recommended; 25–200 MHz allowed; ±300 ppm.
  • SSC allowed: 30–33 kHz modulation, 0 to −0.5% down-spread, ≤ 1250 ppm/µs slew.
  • Tx-to-Rx transport delay difference must be under 5 ns.
  • For a retimer, the off-package link need not share REFCLK; the local UCIe link must.
  • Link Speed settings may be run slower: e.g. the 32 GT/s setting covers 24–32 GT/s by adjusting REFCLK or the PLL divider (only while the link is down).

Differential at 16 GT/s and below, always

At 24 and 32 GT/s the receiver chooses differential or quadrature clocking (advertised in PHY Capability). At 16 GT/s and below, differential must always be used so the link works with designs whose maximum is lower.

Spec§5.5, p.202 · Table 9-47, p.492

Track

On request over sideband, the partner sends a clock-like pattern on its Track lane, aligned to Phase-1: 1010… with half-rate clocking, 1100… with quarter-rate. The receiver uses it to correct slow voltage, temperature and aging drift between data and clock.

Spec§5.5.1, p.202 · §5.3, p.190

6 · Transmitter and receiver

Transmitter

  • Data and clock swing at least 0.4 V. Driver impedance 25 Ω ± 3 (Advanced), 30 Ω ± 3 (Standard).
  • Phase adjustment (deskew) lives on the transmitter; training sets it for best margin.
  • Tri-state outputs in low-power states.
  • 24/32 GT/s EQ (must support; recommended at 16): de-emphasis only, settings 0 dB and −2.2 dB (C+1 = −0.112).
  • 48/64 GT/s EQ (required): 3-tap FIR presets P0–P5. Pick the largest eye; on a tie, the largest C0.

Receiver

  • Clock and data paths delay-matched to reject supply-noise jitter; 2-way or 4-way interleaved.
  • Rx EQ may be used at 24/32 GT/s (about a 1st-order CTLE). At 48/64 GT/s Rx EQ is required with Tx EQ: reference CTLE with at least 7 settings from −6 to 0 dB in 1 dB steps; optional 1-tap DFE with h1/h0 < 0.35.
  • Rx input impedance 50 Ω ± 5 at 48/64 GT/s.

Spec§5.4, p.195 · §5.4.4, p.200–201 · Table 5-9, p.197

Rx termination

  • Advanced: unterminated up to 32 GT/s; terminated to ground for 48/64 GT/s negotiated maximum.
  • Standard, 0.4 V swing: unterminated recommended only up to 12 GT/s at 3 mm, 8 GT/s at 5 mm, 4 GT/s at 10 mm.
  • Standard, 0.85 V swing: 16 GT/s at 5 mm, 12 GT/s at 10 mm, 8 GT/s and below at any supported length.
  • Otherwise terminate to ground.

Spec§5.4.3, Tables 5-10, 5-11, p.197–199

7 · The channel

Eye masks

Checked by channel simulation with ideal (noiseless, jitterless) behavioral Tx/Rx and the 19 largest aggressors. Rectangular mask ≤ 32 GT/s: 40 mV high and 0.75 UI wide up to 16 GT/s, 0.65 UI at 24/32 (with EQ). Diamond mask at 48/64 GT/s: 65 mV and 0.65 UI, searching only presets P0–P5 and the reference CTLE.

Spec§5.7, Table 5-15, p.205–207

Loss and crosstalk (VTF)

Voltage Transfer Function metrics include Tx/Rx termination. Advanced: L(fN) > −3 dB at 4–16 GT/s, > −5 dB at 24/32; crosstalk below a line in loss and below −23/−24 dB. Standard (terminated): L(fN) > −7.5 dB at 4/8 and 24/32, > −6.5 dB at 12/16, with L(0) > −4.5 dB.

Spec§5.7.1, Table 5-17, p.208–209 · Tables 5-24, 5-25, p.235

Lane matching

Allowed channel mismatch around the lane centre: Advanced ±0.03 UI (4–32 GT/s), ±0.06 UI (48/64); Standard ±0.07, ±0.10, ±0.20 UI for 4–16, 24/32, 48/64 GT/s. It is the gap between Tx deskew range and Rx skew tolerance.

SpecTable 5-16, p.208

Bump pitch limits speed (Advanced)

Recommended: 16-column map at 25–30 µm up to 12 GT/s and 31–37 µm up to 16; 10-column at 38–44 µm up to 24 and 45–50 µm up to 32; 8-column at 51–55 µm up to 32 GT/s. Check with the packaging vendor.

8 · Idle, clock gating and BER

Going idle

  • After Valid goes low, keep the clock running for a postamble of 8 cycles: 16 UI half-rate, 32 UI quarter-rate. Then gate it, unless free-running clock was negotiated.
  • Valid held low; data lanes hold the last bit (some may keep toggling like scrambled data without advancing the scrambler).
  • Strobe mode: the parked clock level alternates between high and low on consecutive gating events. Before resuming, drive clock differential low for 1–8 UI. Gated period is a multiple of 8 UI.
  • 48/64 GT/s: the clock always runs (continuous mode).
  • Electrical idle (some training states): Tx/Rx on, data, Valid and Track low, clock parked.

Spec§4.1.3, p.101 · §5.11, p.251–253 · §5.12, p.253

Raw BER sets the error handling

At BER 1E-15 or worse, CRC and retry are required to reach FIT ≪ 1. At 1E-27, parity or CRC with error reporting is enough. 64 GT/s is 1E-12 on both package types.

Spec§5.10, Table 5-32, p.251

Valid protection at 48/64 GT/s

Valid framing uses Hamming-distance-4 encodings. At 48 and 64 GT/s receivers must correct single-bit and detect double-bit Valid errors, which keeps Valid errors from causing frequent retrains.

Spec§5.11, p.253

Spec inconsistency to note at 48 GT/s

§4.1.2.1 (retimer Valid framing) lets receivers at ≤ 48 GT/s retrain on any Valid error instead of correcting. §5.11 requires single-error correction at 48 GT/s. Implementing correction at 48 GT/s satisfies both.

Derived §4.1.2.1, p.101 vs §5.11, p.253

9 · Sideband, AUXCLK and Open Drain

Sideband

800 MT/s SDR on an always-on rail

Data is launched edge-aligned with the strobe's rising edge and sampled on its falling edge. Runs from the always-on supply VCCAON (0.65 V in Table 5-33) and AUXCLK. Setup and hold 200 ps. Same reach as the mainband; Standard Package has an extended-reach option up to 100 mm (60 Ω driver). Redundancy and repair only on Advanced Package.

Spec§5.13, Table 5-33, p.254

AUXCLK

Relaxed clock for the sideband

Any source; 100 MHz recommended, 25–800 MHz allowed, SSC permitted. REFCLK can serve if it is always on. With a frequency-adjusted REFCLK the sideband runs 400–800 MT/s and its 8 ms timeouts scale to 16 ms at 400 MT/s.

Spec§5.13.2, Table 5-34, p.255 · note, p.189

Open Drain

A shared low-latency wire

Optional pin on a package-wide net with one pull-up (5–10 kΩ); any chiplet pulls low (120–240 Ω). Fall to VOL < 100 ns, rise to VOH < 2 µs. Used for UCIe low-latency events such as Fast Throttle, and vendor events.

Spec§5.14, Table 5-35, p.255–256

Sideband-only port

For test and manageability

A UCIe-S port may be sideband only. It uses the same RDI signals as a sideband with a mainband.

Spec§5.7.4, p.248

10 · Repair (Advanced) and degrade (Standard)

Advanced: spare bumps

Each module has 4 redundant data bumps, 1 for clock and Track, 1 for Valid. On x64 the data spares are split two per half (TRD_P0/P1 for lanes 0–31, TRD_P2/P3 for 32–63). Sideband has a redundant data and clock pair. The logical remap is in the Logical PHY guide.

Spec§5.9.1, p.249–250 · Table 5-18, p.209

Standard: drop whole module pairs

  • A degraded link is 1 or 2 modules, never 3.
  • 4-module link: 1 or 2 failed pairs → 2 modules; 3 failed → 1 module.
  • 2-module link: 1 failed pair → 1 module.
  • On a 4-module link with one failure, the other pair in the same half (along the die edge) is also disabled.

Spec§5.7.3.4.1, Table 5-29, p.246–247

11 · UCIe-3D

Design rules

Fit inside the bump area

Circuit and logic must fit under the bumps (at 1 µm pitch, under 1 µm² per lane). No D2D Adapter, no CRC or replay. The PHY is a simple driver/inverter connected directly to SoC logic. Test and debug hooks sit in one common block per chiplet. Lane repair becomes bundle repair run by SoC logic.

Spec§6.2, p.258–259

Timing

Launch on rise, sample on fall

Data and clock buffers are delay-matched, so the data/clock differential is near zero and sampling half a UI later (rise-to-fall) gives the best margin. Unlike the 2D matched architecture, the 3D transceiver avoids its power cost while keeping supply-noise rejection.

Spec§6.3, p.260 · Figure 6-4, p.261

Budget

Everything must fit in 1 UI

Channel eye closure (0.1 UI), pulse-width deviation (0.08), Tx and Rx lane skew (0.12 each), RSS of Tx/Rx random jitter (0.05 each), aperture (0.03) and the Vcc-dependent spread of Tx/Rx data-clock delay (50 ps each, scaled by 1 + α·noise) must sum to less than 1 UI (250 ps at 4 GT/s). With the table's values the sum is about 0.98 UI, so there is almost no slack.

Spec§6.4.1, Table 6-2, Equation 6-6, p.261–263 · Derived sum

Module

x80 bundles, 9 µm recommended

A module is one Tx bundle (x80 + clock) and one Rx bundle. About 0.02 mm² at 9 µm pitch, scaling with pitch squared. x70 also defined. No sideband required; lanes may be assigned to Valid, mask, parity or ECC as needed. Tx swing 0.40–0.75 V. ESD 5 V CDM (≤ 10 µm pitch).

Spec§6.4.2–6.4.3, Tables 6-3, 6-4, p.263–265

Bundle repair calculator

The spec's model: reserve 2k full modules, where k is the smallest value with 1 − Σi=0..k Pi(A·D0) < δ, Pi being Poisson. Check also that large defects are rare: 1 − e−A·D1 < δ.
